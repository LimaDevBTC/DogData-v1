# The opening post: Satoshi Plaza is open

Item 12 of `praca-ajustes.md`. Written to be posted from the project account the
day the new landing goes up. Plates come from `public/landing/plaza/*` (stills of
the live scene, captured from `/city` with the HUD off), so every image in the
post is something the reader can walk into a second later.

House rules used here: no em dashes anywhere in the copy, numbers only where they
are real, and nothing promised that is not already standing.
